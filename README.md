## 📂 Projects

### 🛍️ Customer Behavior Analysis
> *Python · MySQL · Power BI*

End-to-end analysis of 4,000+ customer records from a retail dataset. 
Cleaned and transformed the data in Python, exported it to MySQL, wrote 
10 business queries using CTEs, window functions and subqueries, then 
built an interactive Power BI dashboard.

**What I found:** 73% of customers were non-subscribers (a growth 
opportunity), clothing drove the most revenue, and express shipping 
customers consistently spent more.

📁 [View Project](https://github.com/SOMYAKALA13/customer-behavior-analysis)

### 🏠 Airbnb Seattle — Listings & Revenue Dashboard
> *Tableau*

Explored pricing, geography and revenue trends across Seattle Airbnb 
listings. Built an interactive Tableau dashboard with a choropleth map, 
price-by-zipcode breakdown and revenue time series.

**What I found:** 6-bedroom listings command 6x the nightly rate of 
1-bedrooms, and revenue showed a steady upward trend through 2016–2017.
